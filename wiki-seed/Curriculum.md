# Curriculum

Richmack Georgia is a Georgia Studies learning system, not a map-trivia app. The target age range is **9–14** and the same topic can deepen as the student advances.

## Learning Cycle
```text
Instruction
→ Worked Example
→ Guided Practice
→ Map/Data Lab
→ Quantitative Word Problem
→ Constructed Response
→ Assessment
→ Mastery or Remediation
```

## Current Deep Modules
### Module 1 — Georgia Water Systems
Watersheds, reservoirs, drinking water, wastewater, stormwater, demand, treatment capacity, and downstream effects.

### Module 2 — Transportation & Logistics
Interstates, ports, rail, airports, freight movement, logistics cost, bottlenecks, and location decisions.

### Module 3 — Physical Geography & Mountains
Five physiographic regions, relief, slope, geology, headwaters, mountains, the Fall Line, and geographic constraints.

### Module 4 — Rivers, Lakes & Watersheds
River basins, drainage, reservoirs, upstream/downstream effects, water quality, competing demands, and watershed-scale decisions.

### Module 5 — Georgia History: Place, Change & Evidence
Historical causation, continuity/change, source analysis, geography as a cause or constraint, evidence versus claim, and missing evidence.

### Module 6 — Georgia Economics & Business
Industry location, agriculture, manufacturing, transportation access, labor, markets, specialization, and economic tradeoffs.

## Age-Aware Depth
A reservoir lesson can progress from identifying what a reservoir is (age 9–10), to understanding dams and downstream effects (11–12), to calculating projected demand and defending infrastructure choices (13–14).

## Lesson Quality Standard
A strong lesson should contain a clear problem, explanatory material, vocabulary in context, a worked example, an application task, and assessment that cannot be solved by superficial recognition alone.

## Fictional Scenarios
Fictional numerical cases are allowed for practice, but must be identified as scenarios and not presented as current government measurements.
