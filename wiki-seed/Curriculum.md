# Curriculum

The platform is designed around instruction, worked examples, guided practice, map/data labs, quantitative word problems, constructed responses, assessment, mastery, and remediation.

Current deep modules include Georgia Water Systems, Transportation & Logistics, Physical Geography & Mountains, Rivers/Lakes/Watersheds, Georgia History, and Georgia Economics & Business.

Mastery is designed to emphasize reasoning rather than trivia. Constructed responses can require parent/admin rubric review.
