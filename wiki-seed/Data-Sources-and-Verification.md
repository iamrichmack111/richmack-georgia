# Data Sources and Verification

Richmack Georgia is intended to use attributable, verified information whenever a claim or dataset is presented as real-world fact.

## Source Registry
Sources can record name, agency, URL, subject/category, verification date, and refresh interval.

## Preferred Sources
### Georgia Government
Georgia Department of Education, Georgia EPD, Georgia Geologic Survey, GDOT, Georgia Archives, Georgia Department of Economic Development, and Georgia Ports Authority.

### Federal Government
U.S. Census Bureau, USGS, EPA, and other federal sources when appropriate.

## Freshness Model
### Stable
Location, landform identity, drainage relationships.

### Slow-Changing
Population, employment, industry statistics.

### Frequently Changing
Traffic counts, facility status, monitoring results, current economic measures.

## Fictional Data
Fictional numbers are allowed for math/decision scenarios but must be labeled as such. They should never be described as real operating measurements without a verified source.

## Future Data Health
The admin portal should eventually show source, last verification date, refresh interval, and stale/current status.
